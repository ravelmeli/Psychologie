## Description

Quick test: $100 today, or $110 in a month? Choose before you read on.

Waiting a month to earn $10 is a 10% return in one month. Repeated every month, that would be over 200% a year, which no bank offers. Yet many people take the $100 right away.

Second question: $100 in 12 months, or $110 in 13 months? Now most people pick the $110, even though it's exactly the same wait. Only the word "now" has disappeared. This reflex is called present bias: an immediate reward weighs much more in your brain than the same reward later.

It pushes saving off until tomorrow, makes us buy now and pay later, or empty an account on a whim. To get around it: automate a transfer to savings on payday, decide in advance to save part of your next raise, and give that money a concrete purpose (travel, security, freedom).

So what did you choose at the start? Write 100 or 110 in the comments, and tell us if you changed your mind on the second question.
